FURORIA WORLD REVISION
PLANETARY AND GEOPOLITICAL UPDATE
(Including Ketho, Pexon, Huskarra, Furoria, and Velirum)


------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------

The revised Furoria canon now consists of:

- 6 sovereign nations
- 3 non-polar continents
- 2 polar-zone continents
- 1 global superocean
- At least 7 major languages
- Full canonical inclusion of all five superhabitable planets
  in the Furstar System

This revision replaces the earlier structure of:

- 22 countries
- 35 languages
- 10 continents
- 7 oceans

The updated structure emphasizes:

- clearer geopolitical organization
- astronomical consistency across the Furstar System
- reduced but realistic biodiversity
- stronger cultural identity per nation
- easier long-term worldbuilding stability
- long-term compatibility with future timeline expansions


============================================================
THE FURSTAR SYSTEM
============================================================

The Furstar System contains five confirmed superhabitable planets.

All five worlds are capable of supporting extremely large biospheres
and long-term planetary civilizations.

The system is known for its unusual concentration of superhabitable
worlds within one stellar system.

------------------------------------------------------------

Ketho
First Planet — Currently Unexplored

Diameter: 12,922 km (8,027 mi)
Radius: 6,461 km (4,013.5 mi)

Comparable in size to Earth.

Status:

- Superhabitable environment
- Stable atmosphere and hydrosphere
- No confirmed large-scale civilization
- Limited robotic observation missions only
- Considered one of the least explored planets in the system

Scientific Interest:

Ketho is believed to host a stable biosphere but remains
largely unexplored due to its remote orbital position and
limited exploration priority.


------------------------------------------------------------

Pexon
Second Planet — Largely Unexplored

Diameter: 57,339 km (35,631 mi)
Radius: 28,670 km (17,816 mi)

Approximately 4.5× Earth’s diameter.

Status:

- Vast planetary surface area
- Stable gravity despite large size
- Large oceans and continental regions detected
- Limited exploration activity

Exploration Status:

No confirmed permanent settlements currently exist,
though orbital surveys indicate potentially large
habitable regions.


------------------------------------------------------------

Huskarra
Third Planet

Diameter: 254,840 km (158,360 mi)
Radius: 127,420 km (79,180 mi)

Approximately 20× Earth’s diameter.

Status:

- Major superhabitable civilization world
- Dense population centers
- Highly developed intercontinental societies
- Major scientific and industrial hubs

Huskarra hosts several highly advanced civilizations
with planetary-scale infrastructure and strong
interplanetary research capabilities.


------------------------------------------------------------

Furoria
Fourth Planet

Diameter: 402,800 km (250,400 mi)
Radius: 201,400 km (125,200 mi)

Approximately 31.6× Earth’s diameter.

Status:

- Primary geopolitical focus of this document
- Advanced planetary civilization
- Home to six sovereign nations
- Highly developed planetary infrastructure

Furoria serves as one of the central cultural and
technological hubs within the Furstar System.


------------------------------------------------------------

Velirum
Fifth Planet

Diameter: 191,130 km (118,770 mi)
Radius: 95,565 km (59,385 mi)

Approximately 15× Earth’s diameter.

Status:

- Advanced planetary civilization
- Highly developed scientific and cultural institutions
- Major center for interplanetary academic research

Velirum is widely recognized for its scientific institutions,
universities, and advanced research programs.


------------------------------------------------------------

Huskarra, Furoria, and Velirum collectively sustain
populations far exceeding that of Earth.

Ketho and Pexon remain largely unexplored despite being
fully superhabitable.


============================================================
FURORIA — CONTINENTAL STRUCTURE
============================================================

Furoria contains five continental landmasses and a single
planetary-scale ocean.

Non-Polar Continents

- Aeralith
- Nolupus
- Vixenium

Polar Continents

- Cryvexa (Northern Polar Continent)
- Umbraixe (Southern Polar Continent)

Global Ocean

Slytherian Superocean

This single planetary ocean surrounds and separates the
major continental landmasses and serves as the dominant
marine ecosystem of the planet.

============================================================
OFFICIAL LANGUAGES OF FURORIA
============================================================

Each nation maintains one dominant national language.

------------------------------------------------------------

Furolish

A widely spoken language similar in structural logic
to English but with distinct vocabulary and phonology.

Dialects include:

Anglian (Aeralith)
Ducklanglian (Ducklandia)

Also present as a minority language within
Elyndel's Furolish Isles.


------------------------------------------------------------

Elyndic

Primary language of Elyndel outside the Furolish Isles.

Features phonetic characteristics inspired by
Central and Eastern European language structures.


------------------------------------------------------------

Ducklandian

Dominant language of Ducklandia.

Strongly influenced by regional anthro-duck phonetics
and vocalization patterns.


------------------------------------------------------------

Kitsuni

Official language of Okami-Kitsuniya.

Phonetic structure loosely inspired by Japanese but
fully original in vocabulary and grammar.


------------------------------------------------------------

Luphe

Official language of Coyotrix.


------------------------------------------------------------

Virex

Official language of Virexis.


------------------------------------------------------------

Furorian
Reference Language

No longer spoken as a native language.

Used for:

- historical records
- scientific classification
- continental treaties
- star charts
- maps
- timelines
- interdimensional documentation
- academic translation

Ecological Notes

Exotic pet ownership is heavily regulated.

Uroynxes and Uronyxites were historically kept as pets
in Aeralith and Ducklandia before modern domesticated
species became dominant.

Additional wildlife species exist but overall biodiversity
remains intentionally moderate to maintain ecological balance.

Some species display behaviors that are interpreted
culturally as spiritual or mythological.


============================================================
CIVILIZATION NOTES
============================================================

Governments across Furoria, Huskarra, and Velirum possess
highly centralized administrative capabilities and
advanced technological infrastructure.

Many planets and external dimensions within the wider
cosmic region remain unexplored or classified.

No Furorian nation directly replicates Earth's
political systems.

Most passenger cars are rear-wheel drive by default,
while SUV-equivalent and pickup-equivalent vehicles may
use alternative drivetrain configurations.

Governance, culture, ecology, and technology within the
Furuniverse operate under original worldbuilding systems
independent of Earth.
