FURSTAR SYSTEM
GAS GIANTS AND DWARF PLANET SIZE UPDATE
(Superhabitable Gas Giants, Superhabitable Dwarf Planets,
and the Superhabitable Moon of Furoria)

------------------------------------------------------------
DOCUMENT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

This document provides the updated size classifications for
major outer planetary bodies in the Furstar System.

These include:

- Superhabitable gas giants
- Large outer-system planets
- A confirmed dwarf planet
- Furoria's natural satellite (Furmoon)

Most of these bodies remain unexplored or only partially
studied through long-range astronomical observation.

Some worlds are believed to possess stable atmospheric
layers or orbital environments capable of supporting
superhabitable conditions within floating ecosystems
or specialized biospheres.


============================================================
FURORIA'S NATURAL SATELLITE
============================================================


Furmoon
Primary Moon of Furoria
(Superhabitable Natural Satellite)

Diameter:
8,135.4 KM (5,055.6 MI)

Radius:
4,067.7 KM (2,527.8 MI)

Overview:

Furmoon is the primary natural satellite of Furoria
and is considered superhabitable.

The moon maintains:

- a stable atmosphere
- moderate gravity
- large continental regions
- long-term geological stability

Furmoon remains only partially explored despite its
proximity to Furoria.


============================================================
OUTER PLANETS OF THE FURSTAR SYSTEM
============================================================


------------------------------------------------------------
Jovira
Sixth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
367,549.4 KM (228,385.7 MI)

Radius:
183,774.7 KM (114,192.9 MI)

Overview:

Jovira is one of the largest planets in the Furstar System.

Characteristics include:

- massive atmospheric bands
- extremely strong magnetic field
- extensive ring system
- numerous natural satellites

Scientific theories suggest the presence of stable
high-altitude atmospheric zones where floating
biospheres may exist.


------------------------------------------------------------
Skythos
Seventh Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Outer Superhabitable Planet

Diameter:
28,878 KM (17,943 MI)

Radius:
14,439 KM (8,972 MI)

Overview:

Skythos is significantly smaller than the surrounding
gas giants but still large enough to maintain a
substantial atmosphere.

Current exploration remains limited.


------------------------------------------------------------
Furvaan
Eighth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
448,759 KM (278,846 MI)

Radius:
224,379.5 KM (139,423 MI)

Overview:

Furvaan is the largest known planet in the Furstar System.

Features include:

- enormous atmospheric depth
- extremely powerful storm systems
- vast magnetosphere
- numerous large moons

The planet dominates the outer system's gravitational
environment.


------------------------------------------------------------
Thera
Ninth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
202,372 KM (125,748 MI)

Radius:
101,186 KM (62,874 MI)

Overview:

Thera is a massive outer-system gas giant with a
dense atmospheric structure.

Astronomical surveys indicate:

- large atmospheric storms
- strong internal heat generation
- multiple moons that may be candidates
  for future exploration.


------------------------------------------------------------
Zynthos
Tenth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Outer Gas Giant

Diameter:
154,083 KM (95,741 MI)

Radius:
77,041.5 KM (47,870.5 MI)

Overview:

Zynthos is one of the smaller gas giants within the
outer Furstar System but still vastly larger than
most terrestrial planets.

It maintains a wide ring system and multiple moons.


============================================================
DWARF PLANETS
============================================================


------------------------------------------------------------
Korsh
Eleventh Planet (Dwarf Planet)
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Dwarf Planet

Diameter:
2,998 KM (1,863 MI)

Radius:
1,499 KM (931.5 MI)

Overview:

Korsh is currently classified as a dwarf planet
located in the far outer region of the Furstar System.

Despite its smaller size, Korsh may possess
superhabitable environmental zones due to internal
geothermal heat and a potentially stable atmosphere.

Exploration missions have not yet reached the planet.


============================================================
ASTRONOMICAL NOTES
============================================================

Many outer-system bodies remain unexplored due to:

- extreme orbital distances
- strong planetary radiation environments
- limited exploration infrastructure

Future scientific missions may eventually confirm
additional moons, dwarf planets, or habitable zones
within the Furstar System.
