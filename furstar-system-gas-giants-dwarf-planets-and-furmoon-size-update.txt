FURSTAR SYSTEM
GAS GIANTS, DWARF PLANETS, AND FURMOON SIZE UPDATE
(Superhabitable Gas Giants, Superhabitable Dwarf Planets,
and the Superhabitable Moon of Furoria)


------------------------------------------------------------
DOCUMENT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

This document provides the updated astronomical size
classifications for major outer planetary bodies in the
Furstar System.

These bodies include:

- Superhabitable gas giants
- Large outer-system planets
- Confirmed dwarf planets
- Furoria's natural satellite (Furmoon)

Most of these worlds remain unexplored or only partially
studied through long-range astronomical observation.

Several of these planets are believed to possess stable
atmospheric layers or orbital environments capable of
supporting superhabitable conditions within floating
ecosystems or specialized biospheres.


============================================================
FURORIA'S NATURAL SATELLITE
============================================================


Furmoon
Primary Moon of Furoria
(Superhabitable Natural Satellite)

Diameter:
8,135.4 KM (5,055.6 MI)

Radius:
4,067.7 KM (2,527.8 MI)

Overview:

Furmoon is the primary natural satellite of Furoria
and is considered a superhabitable celestial body.

The moon maintains:

- a stable atmosphere
- moderate gravity
- large continental regions
- long-term geological stability

Despite its proximity to Furoria, large regions of
Furmoon remain only partially explored.


============================================================
OUTER PLANETS OF THE FURSTAR SYSTEM
============================================================


------------------------------------------------------------
Jovira
Sixth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
1,000,138 KM (621,457 MI)

Radius:
500,069 KM (310,729 MI)

Overview:

Jovira is an ultra-massive gas giant and one of the most
dominant planetary bodies in the Furstar System.

Characteristics include:

- massive atmospheric band systems
- extremely powerful magnetic field
- an extensive ring structure
- dozens of natural satellites

Scientific theories suggest the existence of stable
high-altitude atmospheric layers where floating
biospheres or aerial ecosystems could potentially exist.


------------------------------------------------------------
Skythos
Seventh Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Outer Superhabitable Planet

Diameter:
28,878 KM (17,943 MI)

Radius:
14,439 KM (8,972 MI)

Overview:

Skythos is significantly smaller than the surrounding
gas giants but remains large enough to sustain a
substantial atmosphere.

Current exploration of Skythos remains extremely limited,
and most knowledge of the planet is based on remote
astronomical observation.


------------------------------------------------------------
Furvaan
Eighth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
768,382 KM (477,466 MI)

Radius:
384,191 KM (238,733 MI)

Overview:

Furvaan is the second largest known planet in the
Furstar System.

Features include:

- enormous atmospheric depth
- extremely powerful storm systems
- vast magnetosphere
- numerous large moons

The planet exerts a strong gravitational influence
across the outer regions of the Furstar System.


------------------------------------------------------------
Thera
Ninth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Gas Giant

Diameter:
577,892 KM (359,086 MI)

Radius:
288,946 KM (179,543 MI)

Overview:

Thera is a massive outer-system gas giant with a

  exploration targets.


------------------------------------------------------------
Zynthos
Tenth Planet
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Outer Gas Giant

Diameter:
154,083 KM (95,741 MI)

Radius:
77,041.5 KM (47,870.5 MI)

Overview:

Zynthos is one of the smaller gas giants in the

============================================================
DWARF PLANETS
============================================================


------------------------------------------------------------
Korsh
Eleventh Planet (Dwarf Planet)
------------------------------------------------------------

Classification:
Superhabitable Dwarf Planet

Diameter:
2,998 KM (1,863 MI)

Radius:
1,499 KM (931.5 MI)

Overview:

Korsh is currently classified as a dwarf planet located
in the far outer region of the Furstar System.

Despite its relatively small size, Korsh may contain
superhabitable environmental zones due to internal
geothermal activity and the possibility of a thin
but stable atmosphere.

Exploration missions have not yet reached the planet.


============================================================
ASTRONOMICAL NOTES
============================================================

Many outer-system bodies remain unexplored due to:

- extreme orbital distances
- strong planetary radiation environments
- limited exploration infrastructure

Future scientific missions may eventually confirm
additional moons, dwarf planets, or habitable zones
throughout the Furstar System.
