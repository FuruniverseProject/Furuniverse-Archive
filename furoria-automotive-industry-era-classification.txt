------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMOTIVE CULTURAL RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

• All vehicles across history designed for plantigrade feet.
• Pedal layout never changed.
• Mechanical engines remain popular due to heritage.
• Automotive evolution focuses on style and craftsmanship rather than extreme speed.
• Vehicles serve identity, motorsport, and recreation roles in modern society.

The Furoria automotive industry therefore spans nearly one billion years of technological evolution.
