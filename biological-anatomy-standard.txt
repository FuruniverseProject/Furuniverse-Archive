BIOLOGICAL ANATOMY STANDARD
Furuniverse Canon Biological Framework
(Primary Reference: Furoria and the Furstar System)

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE ANATOMICAL STANDARD (ANTHRO CIVILIZATION MODEL)
------------------------------------------------------------

All anthropomorphic citizens across Furoria, Huskarra, and Velirum follow a unified biological template known as the Anthro Civil Standard Body Plan.

This structure applies to:

- Anthro canines
- Anthro felines
- Anthro vulpines
- Anthro lupines
- Anthro mixed-species lineages
- Berdorons
- Primatarins
- Recognized allied anthro species

------------------------------------------------------------
PHYSICAL STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Body Proportions

All anthro beings possess:

- Perfectly proportioned human-like torsos
- Balanced shoulder-to-hip ratios
- Fully upright posture
- Symmetrical limb structure
- Human-standard organ placement (adapted for species biology)

2. Lower Body Structure

- Fully plantigrade leg configuration
- Human-like knees and joint articulation
- Full-foot ground contact while standing and walking
- Human-like arches and heel structure
- Toes structured for balance and stability (not paw-based locomotion)

Digitigrade locomotion does NOT naturally occur in standard anthro citizens.

3. Upper Body Structure

- Fully human-like arms and hands
- Five-digit manual structure (species variation in claw shape permitted)
- Precision grip capability
- Full tool compatibility
- Designed for technological operation and ergonomic efficiency

4. Head Structure

- Animal-shaped head morphology
- Species-specific ears, muzzle, jawline, and eye structure
- Forward-facing vision (civil standard)
- Expressive facial musculature compatible with speech

------------------------------------------------------------
SENSORY ENHANCEMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Many anthro species possess mild sensory advantages that are
naturally integrated into their biology.

Anthro Canines and Anthro Felines commonly possess:

- Natural night vision capability
- Enhanced low-light visual clarity
- Improved motion detection in dark environments
- Slightly enhanced directional hearing

These traits are normal biological adaptations and are not
considered supernatural abilities.

They allow citizens to function effectively during nighttime
conditions across urban and wilderness environments.

------------------------------------------------------------
ANATOMICAL CONSISTENCY ACROSS ERAS
------------------------------------------------------------

The plantigrade, human-like body model has remained biologically stable for trillions of years.

It provides:

- Efficient upright locomotion
- High endurance
- Mechanical compatibility with vehicles and technology
- Stable balance during high-speed transport
- Reduced long-term skeletal stress

All infrastructure across Furoria was designed around this permanent biological standard.

------------------------------------------------------------
EXTENDED SPECIES GROUPS OUTSIDE FURORIA
------------------------------------------------------------

Certain species primarily live outside Furoria (other planets or dimensions) and possess natural life-enhancement abilities.

These abilities are NOT superhero-based.
They are biologically integrated adaptive traits.

Some of these species also possess mild magical or dimensional
abilities that are considered normal biological traits within
their native environments.

------------------------------------------------------------
Peditrans / Therians
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Primarily outside Furoria (varied planets/dimensions)

Peditrans and Therians are known for possessing natural
magical-adaptive biological abilities.

Capabilities:

- Automatic short-range dimensional phase-shift
- Ability to teleport through thin solid surfaces
- Mild magical energy interaction
- Enhanced lifting strength (moderate)
- Limited gliding or controlled aerial descent
- Mild object manipulation enhancement

Transformation Behavior:

Some Peditrans / Therians may experience temporary
biological transformations when certain energy
conditions activate their abilities.

These transformations may include:

- partial energy-based body changes
- temporary morphological adjustments
- dimensional resonance states

These transformations are controlled, short-lived,
and are considered a normal biological function
rather than a supernatural event.

Purpose:

To improve environmental survival and mobility
in unstable or high-energy dimensional regions.

------------------------------------------------------------
Altertherians
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Extra-planetary or interdimensional zones

Altertherians are known for possessing advanced
adaptive biological structures combined with
mild magical capabilities.

Capabilities:

- Controlled wall-phase movement
- Mild levitation
- Enhanced durability
- Light-object telekinetic assistance
- Spatial awareness enhancement
- Limited magical environmental interaction

In rare cases, Altertherians may display mild
temporary transformations when their abilities
interact with dimensional energy fields.

Purpose:

To adapt to unstable, gravity-variable, or
interdimensional environments.

------------------------------------------------------------
Marinians
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Oceanic or aquatic-dominant planets

Capabilities:

- Pressure immunity
- Fluid-environment propulsion
- Short-range water-phase teleportation
- Enhanced breath control (land and water)
- Mild bioluminescent signaling

Purpose:

To improve aquatic civilization efficiency.

------------------------------------------------------------
Reptilians
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Outside Furoria (varied climates)

Capabilities:

- Automatic thermal regulation
- Wall-surface adhesion
- Short-range spatial blink (micro-teleport)
- Controlled glide or limited flight (subspecies dependent)
- Enhanced lifting strength (moderate)

Purpose:

To improve daily mobility and environmental adaptation.

------------------------------------------------------------
BERDORONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Furoria and allied planets

Body Type:

- Human-like plantigrade structure
- Avian-influenced cranial features
- Light skeletal density (within safe range)

Abilities (Mild):

- Controlled glide (not full superhero flight)
- Heightened spatial perception
- Light endurance boost
- Mild kinetic stabilization while airborne

Purpose:

Assist with terrain mobility and vertical cities.

------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMATARINS
------------------------------------------------------------

Habitat: Furoria and allied planets

Body Type:

- Human-like plantigrade form
- Primate-inspired cranial structure
- Enhanced shoulder flexibility

Abilities (Mild):

- Increased grip strength
- Enhanced climbing efficiency
- Mild reaction-time acceleration
- Improved balance

Purpose:

Adaptation to dense urban and forested environments.

------------------------------------------------------------
POWER BALANCE PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------

No species possesses extreme superhero-level abilities.

All abilities follow these rules:

- Enhancement, not domination
- Utility, not combat-based
- Mild to moderate scale
- Biologically integrated
- Designed to improve quality of life

Abilities exist to:

- Improve survival
- Increase environmental compatibility
- Enhance transportation adaptability
- Support multi-planetary civilization

------------------------------------------------------------
COAT COLORS AND VISUAL VARIATION
------------------------------------------------------------

- Limited coat color spectrum
- Controlled genetic diversity
- Reduced mutation frequency
- Stable phenotype inheritance

Anthro and non-anthro animals have distinct coat structures but simplified variation compared to Earth biodiversity.

------------------------------------------------------------
ECOLOGICAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------

The Furuniverse biological system prioritizes:

- Stability
- Balance
- Adaptation
- Long-term sustainability
- Reduced chaotic biodiversity

This framework ensures:

- Ecological resilience
- Predictable evolutionary pathways
- Infrastructure compatibility
- Multi-planetary biological coherence
