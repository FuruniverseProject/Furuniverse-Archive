FURORIA TECHNOLOGY, CIVILIZATION, AND TRANSPORT TIMELINE
(Railroad, Ground Transport, Aviation, Space Exploration, Interplanetary Travel,
Interdimensional Exploration, and Automotive Development)

Calendar System
BZE = Before Zero Event
ZE  = Zero Event and After

Primary Location
Planet Furoria — Central Anthro Civilization World of the Furstar System


============================================================
BIOLOGICAL STANDARD ACROSS ALL ERAS
============================================================

All anthro civilizations across Furoria and related planetary societies share a
universal biomechanical structure.

Biological Characteristics

- Fully upright posture
- Plantigrade legs
- Fully human-like feet with heel-to-toe ground contact
- Balanced bipedal locomotion
- Digitigrade locomotion does NOT exist within standard anthro civilization

Infrastructure, architecture, vehicles, and transportation systems across all
eras were designed around plantigrade biomechanics.


============================================================
PRIMARY POWER AND ENERGY SYSTEMS
============================================================

Across the technological history of Furoria, most advanced technology eventually
relied on crystal-based energy systems.

Core Power Technologies

Crystal Energy Core
High-density energy crystals contained within stabilized lattice chambers.
Used in vehicles, spacecraft, industrial systems, and power plants.

Reaction Chambers
Systems that convert crystal energy into mechanical or plasma energy.

Fusion Systems
Multiple crystal cores feeding a single reaction chamber to increase power.

Hybrid Systems
Combination of crystal engines and electric or gravitational drive systems.

Early Energy Systems

- Mechanical energy
- animal labor
- water power
- wind power

Industrial Energy Systems

- early crystal combustion systems
- hypergasoline crystal ignition engines
- hyperdiesel compression crystal engines

Modern Energy Systems

- crystal electric hybrids
- multi-core fusion engines
- gravitic propulsion systems
- dimensional energy stabilizers


============================================================
EARLY PREHISTORIC AGE
Before 500 Trillion BZE
============================================================

Civilization

- Early anthro species emerge with fully plantigrade anatomy.
- Stable upright posture already biologically established.
- Small proto-settlements appear near naturally stable energy zones.

Transport

- Walking is the dominant form of mobility.
- Primitive load-carrying harness systems appear.

Technology

- Basic tool use
- Early material shaping
- Stone and primitive metals

Energy Sources

- fire
- natural geothermal heat
- primitive mechanical force

Space and Dimensional Activity

- No artificial space travel.
- Natural dimensional anomalies occasionally observed but not understood.
