FURORIA AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY
DRIVETRAIN ARCHITECTURE AND HISTORICAL USAGE

This document explains the drivetrain standards used across
the Furorian automotive industry throughout major
civilization eras.

The primary drivetrain layout across most of Furorian
automotive history is:

Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD)

Front-Wheel Drive (FWD) and FWD-based All-Wheel Drive
configurations do exist, but they are considered uncommon
compared to the dominant longitudinal RWD architecture.

These layouts are primarily used for:

- sedan-equivalent vehicles
- station wagon–equivalent vehicles
- two-door vehicles
- coupe-equivalent vehicles

Pickup-equivalent and SUV-equivalent vehicles almost
always use longitudinal engines with 4x4 / 4WD systems.


============================================================
PRIMARY DRIVETRAIN STANDARD
============================================================

RWD (Rear-Wheel Drive)

Primary configuration across most eras.

Characteristics:

- Longitudinally mounted engines
- Strong performance balance
- Preferred for larger engines
- Compatible with V6-equivalent and V8-equivalent engines

RWD remains the most common drivetrain configuration
throughout nearly the entire automotive history of Furoria.

It is especially dominant in:

- luxury vehicles
- performance vehicles
- long-distance touring vehicles
- executive sedans


============================================================
SECONDARY DRIVETRAIN STANDARD
============================================================

FWD (Front-Wheel Drive)

Status:

Uncommon but present during specific historical periods.

Typical Uses:

- compact sedans
- mid-size sedans
- some station wagons
- some coupes

Characteristics:

- Transversely mounted engines
- improved packaging efficiency
- reduced drivetrain complexity

Manufacturing Origins:

Some FWD and FWD-based AWD models were produced
primarily in:

- Aeralith
- Ducklandia

These vehicles sometimes used:

- V6-equivalent engines
- V8-equivalent engines
- experimental crystal-based power systems

However, these configurations were never the dominant
industry standard.


============================================================
FWD-BASED AWD SYSTEMS
============================================================

FWD-based AWD systems existed in small numbers.

Classification:

Rare to uncommon.

Typical Uses:

- luxury touring sedans
- advanced engineering demonstration models
- experimental high-traction vehicles

Most examples were developed by manufacturers in
Aeralith and Ducklandia.

Despite these developments, RWD systems remained
preferred across most industries.


============================================================
ENGINE ORIENTATION HISTORY
============================================================

Longitudinal Engines

Used primarily with:

- RWD vehicles
- 4x4 / 4WD vehicles
- pickup-equivalent vehicles
- SUV-equivalent vehicles

This configuration remained the most stable and widely
used architecture across Furorian history.


Transverse Engines

Primarily used in:

- FWD vehicles
- FWD-based AWD vehicles

This configuration became more common during
efficiency-focused eras but never fully replaced
longitudinal layouts.


============================================================
HISTORICAL ERA USAGE
============================================================


GRAND TOURING ERA
Continental Prestige Era

5,000,000 BZE – 100,000 BZE

Primary drivetrain:

RWD with longitudinal engines

Characteristics:

- large touring vehicles
- prestige automobiles
- powerful engines
- early high-performance transportation

FWD systems were essentially nonexistent during this era.



------------------------------------------------------------


EFFICIENCY TRANSITION ERA
Efficiency Regulation Era

100,000 BZE – 10,000 BZE

Drivetrain development:

- RWD remained dominant
- FWD and FWD-based AWD introduced in limited numbers

Engine orientation:

- longitudinal
- early transverse layouts

This era marked the beginning of drivetrain experimentation
for efficiency and packaging improvements.



------------------------------------------------------------


TECHNO-REFINEMENT ERA
Electronic Integration Era

10,000 BZE – 1,501 BZE

Drivetrain characteristics:

- FWD systems became more common but still secondary
- transverse engine designs expanded

RWD vehicles remained preferred for:

- performance
- executive vehicles
- large vehicles



------------------------------------------------------------


PRE-MODERN ADVANCED ERA
Pre-Zero Synthesis Era

1,500 BZE – 1 BZE

Drivetrain usage:

- transverse FWD vehicles became moderately present
- RWD still dominant across most vehicle classes

Advanced technologies began appearing in
high-end vehicles.



------------------------------------------------------------


ZERO EVENT
0 ZE



------------------------------------------------------------


EARLY MODERN ERA
Neo-Classic Modern Era

0 ZE – 1800 ZE

Drivetrain transition:

Early to Mid Era:

- some FWD and FWD-based AWD vehicles still produced
- transverse engines used for compact vehicles

Mid Era:

Production of FWD and FWD-based AWD vehicles
gradually ended.

Industry returned primarily to:

RWD longitudinal architecture.



============================================================
SPECIAL VEHICLE CATEGORIES
============================================================


Pickup-Equivalent Vehicles

Standard drivetrain:

4x4 / 4WD

Characteristics:

- longitudinal engines
- heavy-duty chassis
- utility-focused design



SUV-Equivalent Vehicles

Standard drivetrain:

4x4 / 4WD

Characteristics:

- longitudinal engines
- off-road capability
- multi-purpose transport



============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

Across the entire automotive history of Furoria:

RWD with longitudinal engines remained the dominant
vehicle architecture.

FWD and FWD-based AWD systems existed but were
considered uncommon and limited to specific vehicle
segments and historical periods.

By the mid Early Modern Era, most manufacturers
fully returned to RWD architectures for long-term
engineering stability and cultural preference.
